How It Started

Every great idea begins with a simple observation.

Children aren't reading less because they dislike books. They're reading less because they haven't fallen in love with the right story. We saw classrooms filled with potential, parents searching for meaningful learning experiences, and publishers creating incredible books that rarely reached the children who needed them most.
